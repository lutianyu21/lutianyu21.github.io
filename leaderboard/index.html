Fixed-length evaluation. DPLM2 (official): 500 sequences (100 aa: 100, 200 aa: 100, 300 aa: 100, 400 aa: 100, 500 aa: 100). Scores are aggregated from the existing evaluation results.

Repeat. Percentage of residues covered by contiguous motifs of 1–20 residues repeated at least three times. Overlapping repeat regions are merged.

Rep2 / Rep5. Repeated 2-gram and 5-gram fractions: 1 − unique n-grams / (L − n + 1), where L is sequence length. A sequence of length ≤ n receives 0. Values are averaged over sequences and shown as percentages, following qiukeyue’s PDFBench implementation. Metric data ↗
